M3 SERIES IDENTIFICATION CODE:
The M3 series identification code is a 6-digit field. The structure of
the code within the field is as follows:
Position Codes
Position 1 	U- Unadjusted (Seasonally)
A- Seasonally Adjusted
Position 2-4 	M3 Series (* see footnote)
This field contains aggregate level data series, 2-digit SIC major
group series and M3 industry categories.
